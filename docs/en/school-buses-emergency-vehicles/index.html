School buses, emergency vehicles and stopped vehicles

These rules protect children and emergency workers, and the permit test asks about them. The key questions are when you must stop and who must stop.

School buses

Flashing amber lights mean the bus is about to stop: get ready to stop. Flashing red lights (often with a stop arm) mean stop before you reach the bus and stay stopped until the driver turns the red lights off.

On a road where the two directions are separated only by paint, such as a center turn lane, traffic in every lane and both directions must stop. Only when a divided highway has an unpaved median or a barrier do you stop just on the same side as the bus. Worker and church buses with these lights get the same treatment.

Emergency vehicles

When a police car, fire truck or ambulance approaches from any direction with lights or siren, pull as far right as you safely can and stop, but never stop inside an intersection. Stay stopped until it passes or an officer tells you to move. Afterwards, stay at least 500 feet behind it.

Vehicles stopped with flashing lights

On a road with two or more lanes in your direction, if any vehicle is stopped with lights flashing, change lanes so you are not next to it; you may also slow to at least 5 mph under the limit. Give it as much room as you safely can. Do not drive over fire hoses unless told to.

Transit buses and funerals

Yield to a transit bus signaling to pull back into traffic when its rear YIELD sign is flashing. Vehicles in a funeral procession may go through intersections together; do not cut in or join it.

Oregon Driver Manual pages: 55, 56, 57, 58 (Oregon Driver Manual 2026-2027, Form 735-37 (1-26)).

  3. Drive as close as safely possible to the right side of the road and stop

Common questions

Do I stop for a school bus on the other side of the road?

Usually yes. You may keep going only if the road is a divided highway with an unpaved median or a barrier between you and the bus. A painted median or turn lane does not count.

What should I do when I hear a siren behind me at an intersection?

Do not stop in the intersection. Clear it, then pull to the right and stop.

What does flashing amber on a school bus mean?

The bus is preparing to stop. Slow down and be ready to stop when the red lights start.
