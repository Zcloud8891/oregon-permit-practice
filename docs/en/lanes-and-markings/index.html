Lanes and pavement markings in Oregon

Painted lines tell you where you may drive, pass and turn. Remember the basic idea: yellow lines separate traffic going opposite ways, white lines separate traffic going the same way.

Yellow lines

A broken yellow center line allows passing from both sides when it is safe. When the center has one solid and one broken line, the line closest to you decides: broken on your side means you may pass, solid on your side means you may not. A double solid yellow line means no passing for anyone, but you may turn left across it into or out of a driveway or road after oncoming traffic clears.

A two-way center left-turn lane is shared by both directions for left turns only. Enter it just before your turn; driving along in it is illegal. Do not drive in, or turn across, yellow diagonal stripes.

White lines

Broken white lines separate lanes going the same direction; you may cross them with care. A double solid white line means stay in your lane. A wide solid white line marks turn lanes and bike lanes. A solid white line across your lane is the stop line, and a row of white triangles pointing at you marks where you must yield.

Red lanes and bike lanes

Solid red lanes are for buses, light rail or bikes only; striped red areas show where you may cross to turn, park or reach a driveway. Bike lanes have a wide white line and a bicycle symbol and may be painted green. You may turn across a bike lane but must not drive in it to set up a turn.

Lane travel and signals

Drive on the right side of the road. On a road with no center line, give oncoming drivers at least half of it. Signal at least 100 feet before turning or changing lanes when moving in traffic, and check mirrors and blind spots first.

Oregon Driver Manual pages: 18, 22, 23, 24, 25, 38 (Oregon Driver Manual 2026-2027, Form 735-37 (1-26)).

Common questions

What is the difference between yellow and white lines?

Think of the color as telling you which way the traffic beside the line is going: past a yellow line it comes toward you, past a white line it goes your way.

How far ahead must I signal a turn in Oregon?

At least 100 feet before turning or changing lanes when you are moving in traffic.

When may I cross a broken white line?

Whenever a lane change is safe: signal, check your mirrors and look over your shoulder first.
