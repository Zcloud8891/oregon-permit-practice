Oregon speed limits and the basic rule

Oregon law sets default speed limits that apply even when no sign is posted, and it also requires you to drive at a speed that is safe for conditions. Expect several speed questions on the permit test.

Speeds set by law (when no other speed is posted)

  • 15 mph: alleys and narrow residential roads.
  • 20 mph: business districts and school speed zones.
  • 25 mph: residential districts, public parks and ocean shores where driving is allowed.
  • 55 mph: roads and highways that fit no other category.
  • Interstate limits vary, so follow the posted signs.

School speed zones

A school speed zone starts at the SCHOOL SPEED LIMIT 20 sign. Depending on the sign, the 20 mph limit applies when its yellow light flashes, during school hours on school days (7 a.m. to 5 p.m.), or whenever children are present at the crosswalk.

Variable speed signs

Some highways use electronic signs. Black-and-white signs that read SPEED LIMIT are real limits and are enforced like any other. Yellow signs that read ADVISORY SPEED are recommendations, but police can still use the basic rule if you drive too fast for conditions.

The basic rule

On every road at all times, you must drive at a speed that is reasonable and cautious for the traffic, weather, road surface and visibility. You can break the basic rule while driving below the posted limit, for example in heavy fog. If you drive slower than traffic, keep right except when preparing to turn left, and pull over to let a line of cars pass. Racing on any road is illegal.

Oregon Driver Manual pages: 19, 20, 21, 49 (Oregon Driver Manual 2026-2027, Form 735-37 (1-26)).

Common questions

What is the speed limit in an Oregon business district?

20 mph, unless a different speed is posted.

Can I get a ticket for driving under the speed limit?

Yes. Under the basic rule, driving too fast for conditions is a violation even if you are below the posted limit.

Do I have to follow a yellow advisory speed sign?

It is a recommendation, not a limit, but police can enforce safe speed through the basic rule. Slow down when you see one.
